Risk Disclaimer

Important information about the risks of trading and the limitations of the Voyager Analytics platform. Please read this before using any feature.

Last updated: 29 July 2026 Voyager Analytics

Trading carries a substantial risk of loss. Voyager Analytics is an analytics tool, not a signal service, and does not provide financial advice.

1. Trading Involves Substantial Risk

Trading financial markets — including forex, indices, equities, commodities, and cryptocurrencies — involves substantial risk of loss. You may lose some or all of the capital you invest. Do not trade with money you cannot afford to lose, and do not trade with borrowed funds unless you fully understand and accept the associated risks.

Past performance, whether of a market, a strategy, or an individual trader, is not indicative of future results. Market conditions change constantly, and what has worked historically may not work in the future.

2. Nature of Voyager Analytics

Voyager Analytics is a web-based analytics platform designed to help traders visualise and interpret market and economic data, including tools such as COT Positioning, the Macro Matrix, the Correlation Matrix, Currency Strength Meter, the Economic Heatmap, and AI-assisted insights. It is a software product for educational and informational purposes. It is not:

  • A trading signal or automated trade recommendation service.
  • Financial advice, investment advice, or personalised guidance of any kind.
  • A substitute for your own research, judgment, or risk management.
  • A guaranteed or reliable predictor of future price movements.

3. Not Financial Advice

Voyager Analytics is not a licensed Financial Services Provider under South Africa's Financial Advisory and Intermediary Services (FAIS) Act. Nothing produced, displayed, or generated by the platform — including AI-generated commentary, COT signal badges, or macro scores — constitutes regulated financial advice, investment recommendations, or trading signals.

Any information displayed on your Voyager Analytics dashboard is for your own analysis and interpretation. All final trading decisions are yours alone.

4. No Performance Guarantees

Voyager Analytics does not guarantee profits, specific win rates, drawdown limits, or any particular trading outcome. Heatmaps, positioning data, correlation scores, and AI-generated insights can and do lag real market conditions, misread genuine shifts, or go through periods of reduced accuracy — this is an inherent property of all analytical tools, not a defect.

Any historical data, backtests, or examples shown on the platform reflect past conditions only. They are subject to data gaps, revision, and idealised assumptions that rarely hold in live trading conditions.

5. AI-Generated Insights

The AI Trade Insights feature uses Groq's AI API to analyse your own trade and journal history and generate feedback. While insights are generated from your real data rather than invented, they may be incomplete, delayed, or occasionally inaccurate, and should never be treated as a trade recommendation. Always verify important conclusions independently before acting on them.

6. Data and Feed Limitations

Voyager Analytics relies on third-party data providers — including the CFTC (for COT data), central banks and the World Bank (for macro data), and your linked MetaTrader 5 account — for market and economic data. Data may be delayed, revised, or occasionally unavailable due to factors outside our control. Always cross-check time-sensitive data against your broker or primary data source before making trading decisions.

7. Your Responsibility as a Trader

By using Voyager Analytics you acknowledge and accept the following responsibilities:

  • Use any feature, heatmap, matrix, or insight as one input among several — not as a sole basis for a trade.
  • Apply your own position sizing and risk management to every trade, independent of what the platform shows.
  • Never risk more than you can afford to lose on any single trade or trading session.
  • Understand the specific markets and instruments you are trading, including their volatility and liquidity characteristics.
  • Comply with all laws, regulations, and tax obligations applicable to trading in your jurisdiction.
  • Seek independent professional financial advice if you are uncertain about the suitability of any trading activity for your circumstances.

8. Technical Risk

Websites, servers, internet connections, brokers, exchanges, and the MT5 Sync Agent can fail, lag, or behave unexpectedly. Do not rely on any single tool, including Voyager Analytics, as your only risk control mechanism.

9. Third-Party Data and Service Risk

Voyager Analytics depends on third-party data providers and infrastructure, including MetaTrader 5 for trade sync. Voyager has no control over the availability, accuracy, or continuity of these third-party services. Changes to upstream providers may affect how Voyager Analytics functions.

10. Leverage Warning

Many instruments discussed or displayed on Voyager Analytics — including forex, CFDs, and crypto derivatives — are leveraged products. Leverage magnifies both gains and losses. A small adverse market movement can result in a loss greater than your initial deposit. Ensure you understand how leverage works and the risks involved before trading leveraged instruments.

11. Acceptance of Risk

By creating an account or using Voyager Analytics, you confirm that you have read and understood this Risk Disclaimer, that you accept the risks described herein, and that you agree Voyager Analytics is not responsible for your trading outcomes, losses, or financial decisions.
