A Closer Look

The tools that set Voyager apart

New

See what institutions are actually doing

Every Friday, the CFTC publishes exactly how hedge funds and large speculators are positioned across major currencies, metals, and Bitcoin futures. Voyager pulls it automatically and turns raw contract data into something you can act on.

  • 52-week percentile rank shows when positioning is at a real extreme
  • Contrarian signal badges flag when the crowd may be about to reverse
  • Covers EUR, GBP, JPY, AUD, CAD, CHF, NZD, ZAR, Gold, Silver, Platinum, Copper, and Bitcoin
Beta

Every economy, scored side by side

Interest rates, GDP growth, inflation, unemployment, and current account balance for every major economy — pulled from official central bank and World Bank sources, scored, and ranked so you can see at a glance which currencies fundamentals actually favour.

  • Live policy rates auto-fetched from the Fed, ECB, and Bank of England
  • One overall score per currency — no more juggling five different data tabs
  • Updated automatically, so you're never trading on stale fundamentals
New

Know when you're secretly doubling your risk

Trading EURUSD and GBPUSD at the same time feels like diversifying — but if they move together 88% of the time, you're really just doubling one bet. Voyager calculates real correlation from actual price history and warns you when your open positions overlap.

  • Full pair-by-pair correlation matrix, calculated from real daily closes
  • Automatic overlap warnings based on what you've actually traded
  • Single-currency view shows how one currency correlates with every other major
New

A coach that's actually read your trade history

Not generic trading tips — AI analysis built from your real symbols, your real win rate, your real timing patterns. It tells you what's actually working, what's quietly costing you money, and where your next edge is likely hiding.

  • Insights generated from your own trade and journal data — never hallucinated
  • Flags recurring mistakes patterns pulled straight from your journal entries
  • Breaks down performance by symbol, session, and day of week
