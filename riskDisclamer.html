Risk Disclaimer

Important information about the risks of trading and the limitations of Voyager analysis tools. Please read this before using any product.

Last updated: 29 July 2026 Voyager Indicators & Strategies

1. Trading Involves Substantial Risk

Trading financial markets — including forex, indices, equities, commodities, and cryptocurrencies — involves substantial risk of loss. You may lose some or all of the capital you invest. Do not trade with money you cannot afford to lose, and do not trade with borrowed funds unless you fully understand and accept the associated risks.

Past performance, whether of a market, a strategy, or an individual trader, is not indicative of future results. Market conditions change constantly, and what has worked historically may not work in the future.

Voyager indicators and strategies are technical analysis tools designed to assist traders in visualising and interpreting price data. They are software products for educational and informational purposes. They are not:

  • Trading signals or automated trade recommendations.
  • Financial advice, investment advice, or personalised guidance of any kind.

  • A guaranteed or reliable predictor of future price movements.

Any information displayed by a Voyager tool on your TradingView chart is for your own analysis and interpretation. All final trading decisions are yours alone.

4. No Performance Guarantees

Voyager products do not guarantee profits, specific win rates, drawdown limits, or any particular trading outcome. Indicators and strategies can and do produce false signals, lose streaks, and periods of underperformance — this is an inherent property of all technical analysis methods, not a defect.

Any backtesting results shown — whether by Voyager or by individual users — reflect historical data only. Backtests are subject to look-ahead bias, curve-fitting, and idealised execution assumptions that rarely hold in live trading conditions.

5. Backtesting Limitations

When using TradingView's Strategy Tester with any Voyager strategy:

  • Results assume idealised fills at candle open or close — real orders face spreads, slippage, and partial fills.
  • Commission and swap costs vary by broker and are not always reflected in backtests.
  • Historical data on TradingView may contain gaps, errors, or weekend/holiday anomalies that distort results.
  • A strategy that performs well in backtest may perform materially differently in live, forward-tested conditions.

Always forward-test any strategy on a demo account before risking real capital.

6. Your Responsibility as a Trader

By using Voyager products you acknowledge and accept the following responsibilities:

  • Test any tool thoroughly — on demo or paper trading — before applying it to a live account.
  • Apply your own position sizing and risk management to every trade, independent of what any indicator shows.
  • Never risk more than you can afford to lose on any single trade or trading session.
  • Understand the specific markets and instruments you are trading, including their volatility and liquidity characteristics.
  • Comply with all laws, regulations, and tax obligations applicable to trading in your jurisdiction.
  • Seek independent professional financial advice if you are uncertain about the suitability of any trading activity for your circumstances.

A small adverse market movement can result in a loss greater than your initial deposit. Ensure you understand how leverage works and the risks involved before trading leveraged instruments.
