About

Built by a trader,
for traders.

Voyager Analytics is a trading analytics platform built from real market experience — not theory, not a repackaged template, not empty AI-generated boilerplate.

What Voyager Analytics is

Voyager Analytics is a South Africa-based sole proprietorship that builds and operates a web-based trading analytics platform — including COT Positioning, the Macro Matrix, Correlation Matrix, Currency Strength Meter, the Economic Heatmap, and AI-assisted market insights. Every feature is something that was built, tested, and used on real charts before it shipped to the platform.

The platform was started because the tools I wanted didn't exist, or the ones that did were either overpriced, bloated with features nobody uses, or clearly built by people who had never traded a live account.

What this is not

Voyager Analytics is a software analytics platform. We provide data visualisation and interpretation tools — not signals, not advice, not trade recommendations, not managed accounts. What you do with the information on your dashboard is entirely your decision.

Voyager Analytics is not licensed as a Financial Services Provider under South Africa's FAIS Act and does not provide any regulated financial service. If you are looking for someone to tell you when to buy and sell, this is not the right place.

What we offer

A free web-based analytics dashboard with premium features available by subscription — COT positioning, macro and correlation matrices, currency strength, the Economic Heatmap, and AI-assisted insights on your own trade history.

What we don't offer

Signals. Trading advice. Promises of returns. We don't tell you when to buy or sell, we don't manage capital, and we don't guarantee results. No feature does.

How access works

Create a free account and connect MT5 to use the core platform. Upgrade to Monthly or Lifetime at any time to unlock premium features — access applies to your account instantly after payment.

How updates work

The platform is continuously improved. New features and fixes are shipped directly to your account — there's nothing to reinstall or repurchase.

The platform

Every feature has a specific job. Nothing is added for the sake of padding the feature list. The current platform covers institutional positioning via COT data, macroeconomic scoring, cross-asset correlation, currency strength, economic-event impact, and AI-assisted analysis of your own trading history.

New features are only added once they've been used and validated internally first.
