About

Built by a trader,
for traders.

Voyager is a small library of Pine Script tools built from real market experience — not theory, not repackaged open-source scripts, not AI-generated boilerplate.

What Voyager is

Voyager Indicators & Strategies is a South Africa-based sole proprietorship that builds and sells invite-only Pine Script indicators and strategies for the TradingView platform. Every tool in the library is something that was built, broken, fixed, and traded on real charts — tested across multiple markets and timeframes before it gets listed here.

The business was started because the tools I wanted didn't exist, or the ones that did were either overpriced, bloated with features nobody uses, or clearly built by people who had never traded a live account.

What this is not

Voyager is a software tools business. We sell analysis tools — not signals, not advice, not trade recommendations, not managed accounts. What you do with a tool on your own chart is entirely your decision.

Voyager is not licensed as a Financial Services Provider under South Africa's FAIS Act and does not provide any regulated financial service. If you are looking for someone to tell you when to buy and sell, this is not the right place.

What we sell

Invite-only Pine Script indicators and strategies for TradingView. Tools that help you see what's happening on a chart more clearly — trend, momentum, liquidity, session ranges, and market structure.

What we don't sell

Signals. Trading advice. Promises of returns. We don't tell you when to buy or sell, we don't manage capital, and we don't guarantee results. No tool does.

How access works

Buy the tool, send your TradingView username, and access is granted manually — usually within a few hours. The script then appears in your TradingView "Invite-only scripts" tab.

How updates work

All updates to a script are pushed to the same invite-only script. You don't need to buy again — once you have access, you have access to every future version of that tool.

The tools

Every product in the library has a specific job. Nothing is added for the sake of padding the catalogue. The current library covers momentum confirmation, volume-based liquidity zones, trend direction, session range tracking, and candle range theory with fair value gap detection.

New tools are only added when they've been used and validated on my own charts first. The Build Log on the main page records what's changed and what's being worked on next.
