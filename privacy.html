Privacy Policy

How Voyager Analytics collects, uses, stores, and protects your information.

Last updated: 29 July 2026 Voyager Analytics

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Voyager Analytics ("Voyager", "we", "us") collects when you use our website and platform, how that information is used, who it may be shared with, and the choices available to you. It applies to all visitors, free account holders, and premium subscribers.

Voyager Analytics is a sole proprietorship based in South Africa. This policy is written to be consistent with the South African Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA), and with general good practice for handling user data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information — the email address and password (stored securely, never in plain text) you provide when registering. Passwords are hashed and cannot be viewed by us.
  • Authentication data — session and JWT (JSON Web Token) data used to keep you securely logged in. These tokens identify your session, not your identity directly, and expire automatically.
  • Billing information — for premium subscribers, your name and billing email, subscription plan, and transaction history. Card numbers and full payment details are collected and stored entirely by our payment processor (Paystack or Ozow) — Voyager never sees or stores your full card number.
  • Usage data — how you interact with the platform, including pages visited, features used, and general session activity, used to improve the product and diagnose issues.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, device type, and similar technical identifiers collected automatically for security, fraud prevention, and analytics purposes.
  • Support communications — anything you send us directly via email or WhatsApp when requesting support, refunds, or other assistance.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive financial account credentials (such as your broker or exchange login) through the platform. Never share such credentials with us via email or support chat.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account, and authenticate you securely via JWT-based sessions.
  • To provide, operate, and improve the Voyager Analytics platform, including the Economic Heatmap and AI-assisted insight features.
  • To process premium subscription payments and manage billing, via Paystack and Ozow.
  • To communicate with you about your account, billing, service updates, or in response to a support request.
  • To detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, or violations of our Terms of Service.
  • To analyse aggregated, non-identifying usage patterns to improve platform performance and features.

4. AI Features and Data

Certain platform features use AI models to generate market or economic insights. Where your queries or account data are sent to an AI processing service to generate these insights, this is done solely to produce your requested output and is not used to build an advertising profile of you. We do not sell data to AI companies or third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use essential cookies and similar local storage technologies required for you to stay logged in and for the platform to function correctly (for example, storing your session token). We may use limited analytics cookies to understand aggregate usage patterns. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Payment processors — Paystack and Ozow, to process premium subscription payments securely.
  • Infrastructure and hosting providers — companies that host our servers, databases, and backend API, bound by their own security and confidentiality obligations.
  • AI service providers — we use Groq's AI API to process certain queries and generate market/economic insights. Only the data needed to generate your requested output is sent to Groq; it is not used by Voyager for advertising profiling, and any use by Groq is governed by Groq's own privacy policy and data handling terms.
  • Legal or regulatory requirements — where required to comply with applicable law, respond to a valid legal request, or protect the rights, property, or safety of Voyager, our users, or the public.

We do not share your data with third parties for their own independent marketing purposes.

7. Data Retention

We retain account information for as long as your account remains active. If you close your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain certain records (such as transaction records) for legal, tax, or accounting purposes.

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed password storage, and token-based (JWT) authentication rather than storing raw credentials in the browser. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data against unauthorised access, alteration, or loss.

9. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, including POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated personal information, subject to our legal retention obligations.
  • Object to certain uses of your information, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with the South African Information Regulator if you believe your information has been mishandled.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

10. Children's Privacy

Voyager Analytics is not directed at, and should not be used by, anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we become aware that we have collected information from a minor, we will delete it.

11. International Users

Voyager Analytics is operated from South Africa. If you access the platform from outside South Africa, your information may be processed in South Africa or in other jurisdictions where our infrastructure or service providers operate. By using the platform, you consent to this transfer and processing.

12. Third-Party Links

Our platform may link to third-party websites or services (such as our payment processors). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties. Please review their privacy policies separately.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of Voyager Analytics after a revised policy is posted constitutes acceptance of those changes.
