Refund Policy

Refund and cancellation rules for Voyager Analytics premium subscriptions.

Last updated: 29 July 2026 Voyager Analytics

1. Overview

Voyager Analytics is free to use, with optional premium features unlocked through a paid subscription. Because premium access is granted immediately upon successful payment, refunds are handled carefully and on a case-by-case basis. This policy sets out clearly when refunds apply, how to request one, and what to expect.

If you have any questions before subscribing, please contact us first — it is always easier to answer a question upfront than to manage a refund after the fact.

2. Free Trial and First-Time Subscribers

If you request a refund within 48 hours of your first premium subscription payment, and you have made limited use of premium features, we will process the refund promptly — provided the payment has cleared and there is no indication of fraud or abuse. No questions asked at this stage.

3. After the Initial Period

Once the initial 48-hour window has passed, refunds are not guaranteed but will be considered in the following circumstances:

  • Verified technical failure — if premium features do not function as described due to a technical issue on our side, and we are unable to resolve it within a reasonable time, a refund will be offered for the affected billing period.
  • Duplicate or accidental charge — if you were billed twice for the same period, or subscribed by mistake and contact us promptly, we will refund the duplicate or mistaken charge.
  • Premium access never activated — if payment cleared but premium features were never unlocked on your account despite correct login details, we will investigate and either activate access or refund in full.

4. What Does Not Qualify for a Refund

The following are not grounds for a refund once premium access has been active beyond the initial 48-hour window:

  • Dissatisfaction with trading results or market performance while using the platform.
  • A change of mind after having used premium features for an extended period.
  • Failure to read the feature description or plan details before subscribing.
  • Partial-month usage after voluntarily cancelling a subscription.
  • Loss of access due to a violation of our Terms of Service.

No analytics tool, indicator, or AI insight can guarantee trading results. Refunds are not issued on the basis that the platform did not produce profitable trades.

5. Subscription Cancellation

Cancelling a subscription stops all future billing immediately. Cancellation does not automatically trigger a refund for the current billing period already paid, unless required by applicable South African consumer law. Your premium access remains active until the end of the period already paid for.

If a subscription payment fails and premium access is suspended, the subscription can be reactivated by updating your payment details with the payment processor. No refund applies to periods during which premium access was suspended due to payment failure.

6. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

We strongly encourage you to contact us directly before initiating a chargeback or dispute with your bank. Most issues are resolved quickly through direct communication.

If a chargeback or payment reversal is initiated, premium access may be suspended pending investigation. Voyager will respond to all disputes with full supporting documentation. Accounts that initiate fraudulent chargebacks may be permanently blocked from future subscriptions.

7. How to Request a Refund

Send a refund request to visualvoyagebsn@gmail.com with the following information:

  • The email address associated with your Voyager Analytics account.
  • Your payment reference number.
  • The subscription plan and billing date in question.
  • A brief description of the reason for your refund request.

We will acknowledge your request within one business day and aim to resolve it within two business days.

8. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method via Paystack or Ozow. Processing time after approval is typically 3–7 business days depending on your bank or card issuer.

9. Consumer Protection Act

Nothing in this policy overrides your rights under the South African Consumer Protection Act, 2008, or any other applicable legislation. Where this policy conflicts with your statutory rights, your statutory rights apply.

Refund queries: visualvoyagebsn@gmail.com or WhatsApp +27 81 475 8138.
