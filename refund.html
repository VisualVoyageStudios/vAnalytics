Refund Policy

Refund and cancellation rules for Voyager digital tools and TradingView invite-only access.

Last updated: 29 July 2026 Voyager Indicators & Strategies

1. Overview

Voyager products are invite-only digital tools delivered via TradingView. Because access can be granted immediately after payment confirmation, refunds are handled carefully and on a case-by-case basis. This policy sets out clearly when refunds apply, how to request one, and what to expect.

If you have any questions before purchasing, please contact us first — it is always easier to answer a question upfront than to manage a refund after the fact.

2. Before Access Is Granted

If you request a refund before TradingView invite access has been granted to your account, we will process the refund promptly — provided the payment has cleared and there is no indication of fraud or abuse. No questions asked at this stage.

3. After Access Is Granted

Once TradingView access has been granted, refunds are not guaranteed but will be considered in the following circumstances:

  • Wrong product purchased in error — if the correct product can be identified and access substituted at no additional cost, we will do that first. A refund is considered if substitution is not possible.
  • Verified technical failure — if the product does not function as described due to a technical issue on our side, and we are unable to resolve it within a reasonable time, a refund will be offered.
  • Access never received — if TradingView access was genuinely never granted despite a correct username being provided, we will investigate and either grant access or refund in full.

4. What Does Not Qualify for a Refund

The following are not grounds for a refund once access has been granted:

  • Dissatisfaction with trading results or market performance while using the tool.
  • A change of mind after access has been provided.
  • Failure to read the product description, compatibility notes, or limitations before purchasing.
  • Incorrect TradingView username entered by the customer, where access was granted in good faith to that username.
  • Loss of access due to the customer's own TradingView account being suspended or deleted.

No indicator or strategy can guarantee trading results. Refunds are not issued on the basis that a tool did not produce profitable trades.

5. Subscriptions

Cancelling a subscription stops all future billing immediately. Cancellation does not automatically trigger a refund for the current billing period already paid, unless required by applicable South African consumer law.

If a subscription payment fails and access is suspended, the subscription can be reactivated by updating your payment details with the payment processor. No refund applies to periods during which access was suspended due to payment failure.

6. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

We strongly encourage you to contact us directly before initiating a chargeback or dispute with your bank. Most issues are resolved quickly through direct communication.

If a chargeback or payment reversal is initiated, TradingView access may be suspended pending investigation. Voyager will respond to all disputes with full supporting documentation. Accounts that initiate fraudulent chargebacks may be permanently blocked from future purchases.

7. How to Request a Refund

Send a refund request to visualvoyagebsn@gmail.com with the following information:

  • The email address used at checkout and your payment reference number.
  • Your TradingView username.
  • The product name.
  • A brief description of the reason for your refund request.

We will acknowledge your request within one business day and aim to resolve it within two business days.

8. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method via Paystack or Ozow. Processing time after approval is typically 3–7 business days depending on your bank or card issuer.

9. Consumer Protection Act

Nothing in this policy overrides your rights under the South African Consumer Protection Act, 2008, or any other applicable legislation. Where this policy conflicts with your statutory rights, your statutory rights apply.
